You're browsing privately

Lumen forgets this window when you close it. Each private window is separate, so you start signed out of sites here, and other private windows can't see what you do in this one.

Lumen won't save

  • Your browsing history and closed tabs
  • Cookies, site data and cache
  • Permissions you give sites
  • Passwords and form entries
  • These tabs, after a restart

Still visible or kept

  • Files you download stay in your Downloads folder
  • Websites you visit can see you
  • So can your employer or school and your internet provider

The ad blocker, Safe Browsing and HTTPS-only stay on here. Extensions are off, and the AI assistant isn't available: it can't see or use private tabs.
